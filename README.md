# 🍽️ Restaurant Analytics Project

An End-to-End Data Analytics Project using **Python, MySQL, Power BI and Excel**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing restaurant business performance using data analytics. The objective is to transform raw restaurant operational data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

The analysis covers sales performance, store and product performance, customer purchasing behavior, customer feedback, and inventory stock variance.

The project uses a synthetic restaurant dataset representing business operations during 2025.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Evaluate sales trends across different channels.
- Identify top-performing stores and products.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze customer feedback and satisfaction.
- Examine inventory stock variance.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹26.77 Million |
| Completed Orders | 36,798 |
| Average Order Value | ₹727.50 |

*Revenue and order metrics are calculated using completed orders.*

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Total revenue and completed order analysis.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Sales channel performance.
- Average Order Value (AOV).

### 2. Store & Product Analysis
- Store-level sales performance.
- Regional revenue comparison.
- Top-performing products by quantity sold.

### 3. Customer Analytics
- Customer purchasing behavior.
- Customer order frequency.
- Customer segmentation analysis.

### 4. Customer Feedback & Ratings
- Customer feedback analysis.
- Ratings and satisfaction trends.
- Complaint analysis.

### 5. Inventory Analysis
- System closing stock vs physical closing stock.
- Inventory stock variance analysis.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- May recorded the highest monthly sales, while February recorded the lowest.
- Dine-in was the highest revenue-generating sales channel.
- The East region recorded the highest regional revenue.
- Peri Peri Chicken Pizza was the top-selling product by quantity.
- Inventory analysis identified a negative closing stock variance of 1,751 units.
